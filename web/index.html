GET INTO THE GROOVE

A little control.
A lot of impact.

Space plays a kick. Loop repeats on each beat at your tempo. Use headphones or monitors to hear the low end.

Drag a knob up or down. Hold Shift for fine adjustment. Double-click a knob to reset it. Click its number to type a value. Focus a knob and use arrow keys for keyboard control.

The pitch and amp envelope views have draggable handles. The amp handles control hold, sustain, and length; attack remains on its knob. A note-off never cuts the tail: every kick is a one-shot.

Mutate nudges the current sound. Save preset writes a JSON you can load later. Your last sound is remembered in this browser.

Export kick saves the current sound as a mono, 48 kHz, 24-bit WAV. The browser audition tool runs locally; the native VST3 is in the project’s artifacts folder.
